About

I'm a Cloud & DevOps engineer based in Hyderabad with about 2.5 years of experience, most recently at Greatpelican Digital Systems — where I was promoted from intern to full-time engineer within four months and went on to own the entire infrastructure, cloud, and DevOps function solo across four live production platforms.
